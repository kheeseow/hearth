Appliance care · Cleaning

Monthly washing machine clean

A safe monthly routine to prevent odors, detergent buildup, and a dirty filter.

◷ 55 minutes○ Beginner↻ Monthly
laundryappliancesmonthly
◉

△ Safety first

Before opening the filterUnplug the machine from the wall.

Never combine productsDo not mix bleach with vinegar or other cleaners.

Preparation

Before you start

  • ✓Microfibre cloth
    Clean and dry
  • ✓Filter brush
    Soft bristles
  • ✓Machine cleaner
    Maker-recommended amount

Procedure

Five clear steps

1

Remove and rinse the detergent drawer.

Release the drawer according to the appliance manual, then rinse away visible detergent buildup.

Tip: Take a photo before removing it so reassembly is easier.
2

Wipe the door seal.

Gently pull back accessible folds and remove lint, hair, and trapped debris with a damp cloth.

3

Clean the drain filter.

Unplug the machine, protect the floor with towels, and follow the manufacturer’s filter instructions.

4

Run the recommended empty cycle.

Add only the recommended amount of cleaner and select the hottest safe maintenance cycle.

5

Let the machine dry.

Wipe remaining moisture and leave the door and drawer open until the inside is fully dry.
